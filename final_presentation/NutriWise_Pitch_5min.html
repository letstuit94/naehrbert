Problem & solution

The gap we close

PROBLEM

Logging kills the habit

  • Every app = type in every meal
  • Habit dies in weeks → people quit
  • No effortless record of eating
SOLUTION

The receipt logs for you

  • Snap receipt → pantry auto-fills
  • Tap what you ate — 1 tap
  • Weekly gaps + 1 smart pick
  • Honest estimates, not medical advice
Vision
Eating better, zero extra effort
Mission
Turn the receipt into one kind nudge
