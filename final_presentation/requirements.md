# Format
- Presentation of 10 minutes followed by feedback
- **5 min per person = 10 min total** (2 presenters)
- Language: English
- High-level presentation — avoid technicalities as much as possible; speak to stakeholders, not engineers
- Bring your storytelling — explain your project to stakeholders, not to engineers. Keep it simple and short.
- **No technical details in the presentation** — the audience is non-technical (Must have)
- Slides only (no notebooks, Word files, or raw documents)
- This is version 0 of your final presentation

# Presentation Flow
1. Intro
2. Team introduction
3. Story
4. Business slides
5. Product demo
6. Source data — what & why
7. Problem statement + solution
8. How we did it (how-to / approach)

# Content Requirements

## Must have
- AI project name
- Introduce yourself / the team
- Vision, Mission, Slogan
- Target group + pain points
- **Product demo at the start to hook people** (how & why)
- Presentation aimed at a non-technical audience
- Get good data (this is the priority — not a sophisticated webpage)
- Slide numbers

## Nice to have
- Process theory / data flow
- Product live demo
- Outlook + timetable (phases)
- Tech stack
- Backup slides for technical stuff
- Visually appealing graphs
- No highly sophisticated webpage

# Topics to include (from grading guidance)
- Intro slide: project name, title, team member names, etc.
- Business goal and motivation — the problem, the target users, and why it matters
- Success criteria / KPIs — how will you measure whether the product works?
- Project roadmap with milestones — what's done, what's in progress, what's next
- Evaluation metric
- Data collection source and challenges — description of columns, shape, etc.
- EDA with plots
- ML workflow / data pipeline
- Baseline model and its performance
- Thank you slide
- Optional: empty slides referencing next week's work — advanced model, future work, etc.
